# Draft: ingredient-first diet preferences

Status: proposal only, deferred by the owner on 18 October 2026 while Discipline is implemented. The current recipe preference flow remains in place.

Replace recipe-rating onboarding with ingredient categories: staples, proteins, vegetables, fruit, dairy/nuts and seasonings. Keep Love, Okay, Avoid and Never recommend, plus undecided/skipped items. Ask once about the same ingredient across reviewed raw/cooked forms while preserving separate nutrition identities.

Capture cuisine, preparation preferences and spice tolerance separately. Choose and adapt verified recipes around preferred main ingredients, manageable supporting ingredients, existing targets, budget and cooking access. Explicit exclusions remain hard constraints. Keep the standard plan, varied alternatives and independent meal editing.

Recipe feedback after generation would distinguish ingredient dislike, preparation, taste, cost and repetition. Diary learning remains secondary and continuous. Do not restrict every recipe to loved ingredients alone or silently leave a meal empty when preferences make a complete plan unavailable.

This document records the design discussion; none of these proposed onboarding changes are part of the Discipline implementation.
